# Indie launch checklist

A short, practical checklist for the week before you show a small product to the public. It is written for solo makers and tiny SaaS teams. Copy it into your own notes and cross out what does not apply.

The longer version, with explanations and a printable PDF, is on RadarMakers: https://radarmakers.com/launch-checklist

## One week before

- [ ] Write the one sentence that says what the product does and for whom. Say it aloud to someone who has never seen it.
- [ ] Open the product in a private window and finish the signup flow as a stranger would. Note every step that confused you.
- [ ] Check the page title, description and preview image by pasting the link into a chat app.
- [ ] Test the product on the browsers and devices your first users are likely to use.
- [ ] Set up a way to receive replies: a real inbox, not a no reply address.

## Three days before

- [ ] Add a short demo, a screenshot set or a short video that shows the product working.
- [ ] Make sure pricing is stated plainly, including what the free plan does and does not include.
- [ ] Write down what you will do if the site is slow or a payment fails on launch day.
- [ ] Prepare the text for each place you plan to post, written for that place and not copied between them.

## Launch day

- [ ] Post, then stay around to answer questions for several hours.
- [ ] Keep a list of every question and complaint, grouped by theme.
- [ ] Thank people who gave feedback, and say what you changed because of it.

## The week after

- [ ] Read the list of questions again and fix the top three issues.
- [ ] Email the people who signed up and ask one specific question about their first use.
- [ ] Submit the product to a few directories where your users look, and keep track of which ones went live.
